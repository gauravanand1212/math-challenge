Advanced 7th–8th Grade Math Challenge

Try all 10 questions. Enter simplified answers where possible. For word problems, include only the number or dimensions requested.

1. Multi-Step Equation

Solve for x: 3(x − 4) + 5 = 2x + 12

2. Fraction Equation

Solve: x/3 + x/6 = 15

3. Percent Increase and Decrease

A jacket's price is increased by 20% and then decreased by 20%. The final price is $96. What was the original price?

4. Ratio and Proportion

The ratio of boys to girls in a club is 5:7. If there are 84 students, how many are boys?

5. Geometry

A rectangle has a perimeter of 50 cm. Its length is 3 cm more than twice its width. Find the dimensions.

Format example: width 8 length 19

6. Pythagorean Theorem

A ladder is placed against a wall. The foot is 9 ft from the wall and the top reaches 12 ft up. How long is the ladder?

7. Probability

Two fair six-sided dice are rolled. What is the probability that the sum is exactly 8?

Give a simplified fraction.

8. Linear Functions

For y = 4x − 3, find y when x = −2. Then say whether (3,9) lies on the graph.

Format example: -11 yes

9. Consecutive Integers

Three consecutive integers have a sum of 72. What are the integers?

10. Tank Capacity

A tank is 3/5 full. After adding 24 gallons, it becomes 3/4 full. What is the total capacity?
